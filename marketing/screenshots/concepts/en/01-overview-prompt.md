## Generation prompt

Use case: ads-marketing / product-mockup.
Create ONE premium portrait app-store marketing screenshot for AirCapital, the crypto exchange balance monitoring app. This is the first visual concept for approval, not a contact sheet. Target a tall 1242 × 2688 portrait composition.

Reference image 1 is the ACTUAL AirCapital app overview screenshot. Use this as the visible display content inside the device. Preserve the application's design, all meaningful text, fictional numbers, chart, and its visible “Demo · fictional data” badge. Do not invent a redesigned app, extra functions, wallets, trading buttons, or promises of earnings. Enlarge cleanly and keep the small UI text legible. Reference image 2 is the existing AirCapital brand icon; preserve its turquoise/ice-white segmented hexagon identity.

Art direction: restrained, editorial, premium financial software. Deep ink navy #0d121b background, warm white type, mint #78e2c1 and glacial cyan accents. A very soft diffused turquoise halo behind the lower device, fine grain only, tiny unobtrusive hairline orbital arcs near outer edges. No busy coins, no giant Bitcoin symbol, no neon cyberpunk, no confetti, no extraneous cards or badges.

Layout: strong hierarchy and generous breathing room. A small precise brand row near the upper left: existing icon plus “AirCapital”. Beneath it, a very large elegant bold modern sans-serif headline, left aligned, EXACT two lines: “Your crypto.” then “One clear view.” The second line is mint, the first warm white. Below, smaller understated silver-grey copy, EXACT two lines: “Track your exchange balances” then “and how they change over time.”

One large front-facing neutral device mockup occupies the lower two thirds, centred, upright, fully visible with a comfortable bottom margin. It is an anonymous slate: a beautifully crafted thin graphite frame, balanced softly squared corners, fine brushed edge and subtle realistic contact shadow. No branding, camera, notch, Dynamic Island, earpiece, side buttons, home button, system status bar, Android navigation buttons, or gesture bar. Avoid any distinctive Apple/iPhone, Samsung/Pixel or Android industrial-design details. The reference app screenshot fills the inner display without stretching or cropping its navigation bar. Do not make the device resemble a recognisable phone model.

Small discrete text “Demo data” below the device. Everything should feel composed and calm, with app UI as the hero. No App Store or Google Play badges, download CTA, price, ratings, claims of security guarantees, or unsupported feature copy. No additional text. Render sharp, professionally finished typography and faithful actual app content.
