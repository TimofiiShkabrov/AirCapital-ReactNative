## Full description

Your crypto is on different exchanges. Your overview does not have to be.

AirCapital brings balances from Binance, Bybit, OKX, BingX and Gate.io into one clear view. Check your total portfolio value, compare accounts and see how your capital changes over time.

• Balances and asset allocation across connected accounts
• Portfolio history, growth and declines
• Deposits and withdrawals alongside balance changes
• Statistics and period comparisons
• Demo with fictional data, no exchange connection needed

Connect with read-only API keys stored securely on your device. Optional biometric protection helps keep your overview private. Usage analytics are optional; balances and API keys are not sent to Analytics.

AirCapital is a monitoring tool. It does not trade, initiate withdrawals or hold your funds. Data coverage and availability depend on each exchange API.
