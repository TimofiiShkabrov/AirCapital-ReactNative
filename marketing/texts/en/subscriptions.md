## Localized benefits

Pro includes unlimited exchange account connections. Two accounts, all their wallets and security features remain free.

- Full recorded history
- Detailed statistics and period comparisons
- CSV portfolio reports
- Capital change alerts

Deposits and withdrawals are entered manually. Performance is calculated only after you confirm the records are complete.

Alerts are checked when balances refresh while the app is open. The threshold is measured from the first reading or last alert. This is not background monitoring.

## Localized billing disclosure

The full monthly or yearly price is charged at purchase. Your subscription renews automatically unless cancelled in your app store before renewal. Cancellation stops future renewals; access continues until the paid period ends.
