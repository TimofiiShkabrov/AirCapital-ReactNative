# AirCapital 2.5.0 — review notes

AirCapital is a read-only monitor of balances on Binance, Bybit, OKX, BingX and Gate.io. It does not execute trades, initiate withdrawals or hold funds. No AirCapital account or login exists. The download is free; AirCapital Pro is an optional auto-renewing subscription (monthly and yearly) that unlocks unlimited exchange accounts, full recorded history, detailed statistics, CSV reports and local capital alerts. Every screen used by the demo is available without purchasing.

On a fresh install, accept or decline optional analytics, then tap "Explore the demo" on the overview screen. The demo uses fictional balances and history and shows the overview, exchanges, wallet balances and statistics. Settings, language selection and connection guides are accessible without an account. The paywall is reachable from Settings > "AirCapital" > "Choose your plan", from the "All time" period on the overview, or from the "AirCapital Pro" card; it shows both plans with store prices, Restore Purchases, and links to the privacy policy and terms. Biometric protection is optional and disabled by default.

Live exchange synchronisation requires a user-owned read-only exchange API key. Trading and withdrawal permissions are not accepted. The demo does not perform authenticated exchange requests and does not expose every live-account detail, cash-flow or export action. No reusable live exchange test credentials are included in this submission.

Please do not include API keys or personal portfolio data in feedback or screenshots. Privacy policy: https://aircapital.app/privacy. Local data deletion instructions: https://aircapital.app/data-deletion. Contact: timofii.shkabrov@gmail.com.
