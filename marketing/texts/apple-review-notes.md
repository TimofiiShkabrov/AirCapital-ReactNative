# AirCapital 2.4.7 — review notes

AirCapital is a read-only monitor of balances on Binance, Bybit, OKX, BingX and Gate.io. It does not execute trades, initiate withdrawals or hold funds. No AirCapital account, subscription or in-app purchase is needed.

On a fresh install, accept or decline optional analytics, then tap "Explore the demo" on the overview screen. The demo uses fictional balances and history and shows the overview, exchanges, wallet balances and statistics. Settings, language selection and connection guides are accessible without an account. Biometric protection is optional and disabled by default.

Live exchange synchronisation requires a user-owned read-only exchange API key. Trading and withdrawal permissions are not accepted. The demo does not perform authenticated exchange requests and does not expose every live-account detail, cash-flow or export action. No reusable live exchange test credentials are included in this submission.

Please do not include API keys or personal portfolio data in feedback or screenshots. Privacy policy: https://aircapital.app/privacy. Local data deletion instructions: https://aircapital.app/data-deletion. Contact: timofii.shkabrov@gmail.com.
