# Cookies and analytics

Updated: 2026-09-07

Essential preferences, optional measurement and how to change your choice.

## Website preferences

The site uses browser local storage to remember your language (aircapital.website.language) and analytics choice (aircapital.web.analytics.v1). A legacy language preference may be read when present. Language remains until changed or cleared; analytics consent expires after 180 days. These preferences are used to respect your choices and are not advertising identifiers.

## Optional Google Analytics

Only after acceptance, our website loads the Google tag for G-BLV9ZEBKW9. Google may set _ga and _ga_* cookies to distinguish browsers and sessions. Their default expiry can be up to two years and may be renewed on use. They support visit, engagement and demo-interaction measurement. Advertising storage, ad personalisation and Google signals are disabled.

Before acceptance, we do not load the Google tag or send analytics events to Google. Rejecting analytics keeps the site and demo usable. Hosting requests needed to deliver the page still occur.

## Change or withdraw your choice

Use Cookie settings in the footer, then choose Reject analytics or Accept analytics. Rejection stops further collection and removes the site’s _ga cookies where the browser permits. You can also clear cookies and local storage through your browser. Removing the consent record causes the site to ask again.

Withdrawing consent does not automatically delete previously received analytics events. See the privacy policy and deletion guide for requests concerning historical data.

- [Privacy policy](https://aircapital.app/privacy)
- [Data deletion](https://aircapital.app/data-deletion)

## iOS and Android

The native apps use Google Analytics for Firebase, not website cookies. Collection starts only after you opt in. You can switch it off in the app’s Settings. Balances and exchange API keys are not sent to Analytics. The full privacy policy explains device identifiers and retention.
